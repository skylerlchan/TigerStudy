### Ascendancy of Whiteness (Ray Chow)

- Formerly marginalized groups move toward hegemonic status

- Modern examples of queer white patriarchy
- Powerful figures like Tim Cook, Sam Altman, Pete Buttigieg- Competitive edge over traditional model minorities- Approximate but never supplant heteronormative structures

### Biopolitics vs. Necropolitics

- Biopolitics (Foucault)
- Shift from individual to population-level thinking- State optimizes life for entire populations- Replaced sovereign power of life/death over individuals
- Necropolitics (Mbembe)
- Certain populations must die for others to live- Israel/Palestine as prime example
- Spatial apartheid configuration- Underground Palestinian roads vs. above-ground Israeli highways- AIDS crisis illustration
- Early neglect of marginalized populations- Current Western investment vs. African continent disparity
