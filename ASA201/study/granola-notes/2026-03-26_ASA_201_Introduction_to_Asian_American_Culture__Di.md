# ASA 201: Introduction to Asian American Culture & Difference

**Date:** 2026-03-26


## Summary

### Japanese American Internment as Data Project

- Noguchi’s garden design at Poston internment camp
- Landscape architecture aimed to prove accessibility and humanity- Exterior aesthetics used to improve group perception- Connection to City Beautiful movement - using aesthetics for moral improvement
- Question of resistance vs. pandering to hegemonic system
- Aestheticizing incarceration as tool for psychological relief- Allowed people to transcend material conditions even when unable to escape them- Debate whether this constituted genuine resistance or accommodation

### Modern Parallels and Yellow Peril Evolution

- Asian Americans in tech now seen as ideal coders/data workers
- Desirable traits: intelligence, political quietness, system manageability- Sanitized success stories from internment experience
- New yellow peril targeting Chinese intellectuals
- China as technology/security threat- Chinese nationals in tech face scrutiny and targeting- Survival requires pandering to existing government systems

### Historical Context of Data and Race

- Norbert Wiener’s classified “Yellow Peril” document
- Father of information age alongside Shannon- Document nickname referenced yellow cover, not content- Encoded racial sentiment despite Wiener’s pro-Chinese stance
- Life Magazine’s “How to Tell Japanese from Chinese” algorithm
- Pseudoscientific data to distinguish ethnicities- Aimed to protect Chinese Americans from anti-Japanese violence- Early example of race made algorithmic despite impossibility

### Internment as Humanitarian Data Collection

- Administrators were progressive reformers, not intentional dehumanizers
- John Collier: career working with Native Americans- Others worked in Hawaii with native populations- Well-intentioned people seeking to “uplift” minority populations
- Alexander Leighton conducted first sentiment analysis
- Systematic study of human emotion and culture- Recruited 9 Japanese Americans as participant observers/co-authors- Applied engineering concepts to understand human behavior

### Information Theory and Social Engineering

- Claude Shannon and Warren Weaver’s communication theory
- Universal system to encode chaos of real world into common system- Centered noise and unknowability as foundation for knowledge- Content-indifferent technique applicable to any system needing revelation
- Bridge between technical concepts and human unpredictability
- Can emotion be systematized with right methods?- Foundation of social engineering applied to race- Asian American body as proof of concept for these principles

### Research Methodology and Broader Implications

- Cross-disciplinary approach bridging archives
- Literature, art, and digital games as research apertures- Connections across disparate histories without strict causation- Focus on knowledge formation rather than individual intent
- Contemporary applications
- Pokemon Go data used by Niantic for robotics training- China’s social credit system using similar data collection frameworks- Techniques developed in West now applied globally to manage populations
