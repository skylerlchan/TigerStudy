### Modern Connections

- Labor dynamics transformation

- Model minority myth as continuation
- Economic efficiency stereotype persists- Structural control through ideological discipline vs. physical containment
- Largest undocumented labor class in US still Chinese
- Often in massage parlors with little worker protection
