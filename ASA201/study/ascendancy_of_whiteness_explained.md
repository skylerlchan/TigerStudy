# WTF is "Ascendancy of Whiteness"?

## The Simple Version

**Ascendancy of whiteness** = Whiteness gets more powerful through *inclusion and diversity*, not just through exclusion and racism.

**The mindfuck**: When institutions say "we're so diverse!" and include some people of color, that actually *strengthens* white dominance rather than challenging it.

## The Slightly Less Simple Version

Rey Chow (the scholar Puar draws from) argues that whiteness maintains power in the modern world through **liberal strategies** rather than just brutal racism:

1. **Include some minorities** (but only the "good" ones who play by white rules)
2. **Celebrate diversity** (which makes whiteness look innocent and progressive)
3. **Let minorities "represent" their culture** (in tokenizing ways)
4. **Meanwhile, white people stay in power** and get to feel good about themselves

The result: Whiteness becomes **invisible** as the norm, even as it gets stronger.

## The Key Quote from Puar

> "The ascendancy of whiteness is a description of biopolitics proffered by Rey Chow, who links **the violence of liberal deployments of diversity and multiculturalism** to the 'valorization of life' alibi that then **allows for rampant exploitation of the very subjects included in discourses of diversity** in the first instance."

**Translation**:
- Diversity programs are violent? YES
- They exploit the very people they claim to help? YES
- This happens *through* inclusion, not despite it? YES

## How It Works: The Mechanism

### Step 1: Liberal Inclusion
"We need diversity!" → Some people of color get included in institutions

### Step 2: The Rules Stay White
But they have to:
- Act "professional" (white middle-class norms)
- Not be "too angry" about racism
- Be grateful for the opportunity
- Prove they're "one of the good ones"

### Step 3: This Fractures Solidarity
Middle-class people of color who "made it" get separated from working-class people of color. No cross-class racial solidarity.

### Step 4: Whiteness Wins
- White people: "Look how diverse we are! Racism is over!"
- White power structures: unchanged
- White people: feel innocent, progressive
- Whiteness: more powerful than ever because it's now invisible

## Puar's Addition: The Queer Version

Puar applies this to queerness:

> "The ascendancy of whiteness is not strictly delimited to white subjects, though it is bound to multiculturalism as defined and deployed by whiteness. **Neither is the ascendancy of whiteness strictly bound to heterosexuality**, though it is bound to heteronormativity. [...] **The project of whiteness is assisted and benefited by homosexual populations** that participate in the same identitarian and economic hegemonies."

**Translation**:
- Some gay people (usually white, or acting white) now get included in American nationalism
- This makes whiteness look progressive: "We accept gay people!"
- But it requires distancing from "bad" queers (POC, Muslims, poor people, non-respectable queers)
- Result: **Homonationalism** = gay people helping white supremacy

## Real World Examples

- Meanwhile: Most LGBTQ employees are white, middle-class

- Company still donates to anti-LGBTQ politicians
- **Result**: Whiteness (and capitalism) wins, looks progressive

- Middle East/Africa: "They're so backwards and homophobic!"
- This justifies: wars, imperialism, sanctions

- **Result**: White western nations use gay rights to claim superiority

- That professor has to:
  - Not make white people uncomfortable
  - Do all the "diversity work" (unpaid)
  - Represent all Black/queer people
  - Be grateful, not angry
- White professors: same power, same comfort, feel progressive
- **Result**: University looks diverse, white power unchanged

## The Paradox

**Traditional white supremacy**: "No Black people allowed"
**Ascendancy of whiteness**: "We love diversity! Come join us! (But only if you act like us, don't challenge us, and make us feel good)"

The second one is actually *more powerful* because:
1. It's invisible (looks like progress)
2. It splits up resistance (some minorities "make it," others don't)
3. It makes white people feel innocent ("But we're not racist! Look how diverse we are!")

## Why It's "Ascendancy" (Going Up) Not "Supremacy" (Domination)

Chow uses "ascendancy" instead of "supremacy" because:
- **Supremacy** = obvious domination, exclusion (easier to fight)
- **Ascendancy** = rising, gaining power *through* liberal inclusion (sneaky, harder to fight)

It's the difference between:
- "We're keeping them out" (supremacy)
- "We're letting them in!" (ascendancy) → but on our terms, maintaining our power

## Connection to Biopolitics

Chow links this to Foucault's **biopower** = power over who lives and dies, who thrives and who suffers.

The ascendancy of whiteness works through "valorization of life" = celebrating and protecting *some* lives while abandoning others.

**Applied to queerness**:
- "Good gays" (white, monogamous, respectable) → valued, protected, included
- "Bad gays" (POC, poor, trans, sex workers, non-western) → abandoned, criminalized, deported

The inclusion of "good gays" makes whiteness look progressive while *increasing* violence against "bad gays."

## How to Talk About This in Class

### The Basic Move
"Puar builds on Rey Chow's concept of the ascendancy of whiteness to demonstrate how queerness can be implicated in projects of white supremacy—not through exclusion, but through **liberal inclusion** that actually strengthens white dominance."

### When Someone Talks About Progress
"That's an important point about LGBTQ rights gains, but I'm thinking about how those gains might be operating within what Chow calls the ascendancy of whiteness. When only certain queers—typically white, middle-class, respectable ones—gain recognition and rights, that can actually strengthen white power structures rather than dismantling them."

### When Someone Talks About Diversity
"I want to sit with Chow's argument that diversity itself can be a mechanism for the ascendancy of whiteness. It's not that diversity is bad, but the *deployment* of diversity as liberal alibi—where inclusion becomes the end goal rather than structural transformation—can exploit the very subjects it claims to uplift."

### The Complication
"What's particularly insidious about the ascendancy of whiteness is that it works *through* what look like progressive moves. So we can't simply celebrate inclusion or representation without asking: inclusion into what? On whose terms? And who remains excluded?"

## TL;DR

**Ascendancy of whiteness** = White power getting stronger through diversity/inclusion rather than through obvious racism.

**How it applies to queerness** = Some gay people get included in nationalism (homonationalism), which makes whiteness look progressive but actually strengthens white supremacy.

**Why it's sneaky** = Looks like progress, feels like progress, but maintains white dominance.
