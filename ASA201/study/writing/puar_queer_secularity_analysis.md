# Puar Analysis: Queer Secularity and the Regulatory Function of Queerness

## The Core Paradox: Transgression as Regulation

**THE LIGHTBULB MOMENT**: Queerness claims to be anti-normative, but *becomes its own regulatory regime* through the very performance of transgression.

> "Queerness here is the modality through which 'freedom from norms' becomes a **regulatory queer ideal** that demarcates the ideal queer."

Translation: The demand to be "free from norms" is itself a norm that polices who counts as properly queer. If you're not transgressive enough, you're not queer enough.

## Three Mechanisms of Regulatory Queerness

Puar identifies how queerness functions as a biopolitical regulatory project:

### 1. Transgression as Discipline

> "Queerness as automatically and inherently transgressive **enacts specific forms of disciplining and control**, erecting celebratory queer liberal subjects folded into life (queerness as subject) against the sexually pathological and deviant populations targeted for death (queerness as population)."

**JAW-DROPPING INSIGHT**: Claiming queerness is "automatically transgressive" creates a hierarchy where:
- The "good queers" = transgressive, secular, individualistic, free from norms
- The "bad queers" = those marked as pathological (often racialized, religious, non-western)

This *isn't* just about including some and excluding others—it's about **creating populations worthy of life vs. populations marked for social death**.

### 2. Regulatory Transgression as Alibi for Other Oppressions

> "Within that orientation of regulatory transgression, queer operates as an **alibi for complicity with all sorts of other identity norms**, such as nation, race, class, and gender, unwittingly lured onto the ascent toward whiteness."

**THE TRAP**: If you're queer and transgressive, you get a free pass on examining your whiteness, nationalism, class privilege, etc. Your queerness becomes proof you're on the "right side" of power.

### 3. The Impossibility of Self-Awareness

> "Allowing for complicities signals not the failure of the radical, resistant, or transgressive; rather it points to the impossibility of any pure space from which to critique."

## Queer Secularity: The Religious Exception

**THE BOMB PUAR DROPS**: Queer secularity is the presumption that "proper" queerness *requires* secularism—that religion and queerness are fundamentally incompatible.

> "**Queer secularity** demands a particular transgression of norms, religious norms that are understood to otherwise bind that subject to an especially egregious interdictory religious frame. The queer agential subject can only ever be fathomed outside the norming constrictions of religion."

### The Muslim Question

This becomes especially violent when applied to Muslims:

> "For queer Arabs and Muslims the either/or plight thickens: **queer secularity understands observance of religious creed, participation in religious public spaces and rituals, devotion to faith-based or spiritual practices, and simply residence within an Islamic nation-state** [...] **as marks of subjugated and repressed sexuality void of agency**."

**TRANSLATION**: If you're a practicing Muslim, queer secularity assumes you *cannot* have sexual agency. Your queerness is either:
1. Not real (you're just repressed)
2. Heroic (you've escaped the backwards religion)
3. Impossible (Islam and queerness can't coexist)

> "The agency of all queer Muslims is invariably evaluated through the **regulatory apparatus of queer liberal secularity**."

## The Double Bind: Freedom as Unfreedom

Sara Ahmed (quoted by Puar):
> "The idealization of movement, or transformation of movement into a fetish, **depends on the exclusion of others who are already positioned as not free in the same way**."

**THE DEVASTATING POINT**: The celebration of queer "freedom" and "mobility" requires creating a category of unfree others—typically:
- Immigrants who can't move freely
- People of color "bound" by family or community
- Religious people "trapped" by faith
- Working-class people without material resources for queer cosmopolitanism

> "Individual freedom becomes the barometer of choice in the valuation, and ultimately, **regulation**, of queerness."

## How Queer Movements Create New Norms/Exclusions

### The Whiteness Ascendancy

> "This brand of homosexuality operates as a **regulatory script not only of normative gayness**, queerness, or homosexuality, **but also of the racial and national norms** that reinforce these sexual subjects. There is a commitment to the global dominant ascendancy of whiteness."

**Puar's term**: HOMONATIONALISM—the incorporation of certain homosexual subjects into nationalist projects, *through* their queerness, not despite it.

### The Elite Cosmopolitan Formation

> "Queerness can be an **elite cosmopolitan formulation** contingent upon various regimes of mobility. Ironically, 'those that have access' to such cultural capital and material resources may constitute the very same populations that many would accuse of assimilation."

**THE IRONY**: The most "transgressive" queers might be the most privileged—they have the resources to perform transgression as lifestyle.

### The Violence of Liberal Queerness

> "**Violence, especially of the liberal varieties, is often most easily perpetrated in the spaces and places where its possibility is unequivocally denounced**."

**JAW-DROP**: The places that claim to be most against violence (liberal queer spaces) are often where violence happens most easily—*because* they're so convinced of their own righteousness.

## The Regulatory Binary: Assimilation vs. Transgression

Puar argues this binary is itself regulatory:

> "What is at stake in defusing queer liberal binaries of **assimilation and transgression, secularity and religiosity**?"

Both sides of these binaries enforce norms:
- **Transgressive queers**: Must be secular, mobile, individualistic, breaking all norms
- **Assimilationist queers**: Must be respectable, monogamous, consumer-citizens

Both exclude:
- Queer people with religious commitments
- Queer people tied to family/community
- Working-class queers without cosmopolitan mobility
- Queer people of color navigating multiple oppressions

### The Intersectionality Trap

Even queer of color critique can become regulatory:

> "Queer of color and queer immigrant communities (not to mention queer of color critique) are **always beyond reproach**, an untenable position [...] thus obfuscating any of their own conservative proclivities. Conversely, it also holds queer of color organizing and theorizing to **impossible standards and expectations**, always beholden to spaces and actions of resistance, transgression, subversion."

**THE TRAP**: Either queer of color people:
1. Can do no wrong (impossible, denies agency)
2. Must always be perfectly radical (impossible standard)

## The Queer Secular Subject vs. The Terrorist

Puar's most chilling analysis: post-9/11, the "queer terrorist" becomes imaginable, which disrupts queer secularity:

> "The **(white) secular norms by which queerness abides** contributes greatly to (racist) Islamo- and homophobic representations of terrorists. That is, the queer transgressive subject accrues its legitimacy and currency at this historical juncture through an **inability to disentangle these representations via a broader articulation of queer religiosity**."

**TRANSLATION**: White secular queerness gains legitimacy by distancing itself from the "religious terrorist"—but this means it *cannot* acknowledge queer religious people without undermining its own legitimacy.

> "**Queer secularity is constitutive of and constituted by** the queer autonomous liberal subject against and through the reification of the very pathological irrational sexualities that are endemic to discourses of terrorist culpability."

## The Biopolitical Stakes

This isn't just about inclusion/exclusion—it's about **life and death**:

> "Queerness as automatically and inherently transgressive enacts specific forms of disciplining and control, erecting celebratory queer liberal subjects **folded into life** (queerness as subject) against the sexually pathological and deviant populations **targeted for death** (queerness as population)."

Regulatory queerness decides:
- Who is worthy of life, recognition, citizenship
- Who is marked for social death, deportation, violence

## The Way Forward: Resisting Resistance

> "If we are to **resist resistance**, reading against these binaries to foreground a broader array of power affiliations and disaffiliations that are often rife with contradiction should not provide ammunition to chastise, but rather generate greater room for self-reflection."

**PUAR'S CALL**: Stop using transgression as a measure of radical politics. Recognize that:
1. We are all complicit in various power structures
2. There is no pure position from which to critique
3. Contradiction is inevitable
4. The goal isn't purity but self-awareness about our own regulatory impulses
