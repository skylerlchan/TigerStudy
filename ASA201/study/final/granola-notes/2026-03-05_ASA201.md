### Racial Triangulation Framework Analysis

- Traditional model positions groups hierarchically:- White at top (insider status)- Asian as foreign outsiders but valorized above Black- Black as inferior insiders at bottom

- New Orleans East case shows conscious effort to resist this positioning

- Environmental racism concerns sparked joint Vietnamese-Black protests

- Mutual recognition of resilience and commitment to rebuilding
