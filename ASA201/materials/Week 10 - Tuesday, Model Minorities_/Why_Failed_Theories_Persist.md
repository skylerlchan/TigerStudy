# Why Failed Theories Dominate: A Real Answer

## The Question:

**"If EMH failed to predict 2008 and HCT can't explain discrimination, why do these Nobel Prize theories still dominate despite failing their own empirical standards?"**

---

## The Answer: 7 Interlocking Mechanisms

### 1. **THEORIES DON'T NEED TO BE RIGHT - THEY NEED TO BE USEFUL (To Power)**

**The brutal truth:**
Economic theories persist not because they're accurate, but because they serve political and economic interests.

**EMH serves:**
- Financial industry (justifies high fees for "efficient" markets)
- Deregulation advocates (markets are rational, don't need oversight)
- Wealth concentration (market outcomes are "efficient" therefore just)

**HCT serves:**
- Anti-welfare advocates (poverty = bad investment, not structural)
- Meritocracy ideology (success = effort, not privilege)
- Racial hierarchy maintenance (Asian success "proves" system works)

**The mechanism:**
- Powerful institutions fund research that confirms their interests
- Think tanks promote these theories
- Politicians cite them to justify policies
- Media treats them as "neutral science"
- **The theory's utility to power > its empirical accuracy**

**Historical parallel:**
- Phrenology persisted because it justified racism
- Eugenics persisted because it justified inequality
- Trickle-down economics persists despite failing for 40 years
- **Utility to power beats empirical accuracy**

---

### 2. **INSTITUTIONAL PATH DEPENDENCE (Can't Un-Nobel Prize Someone)**

**The problem:**
Once a theory becomes institutionalized, it's nearly impossible to dislodge.

**The path:**
1. Becker/Schultz develop HCT (1960s-70s)
2. Gets published in top journals
3. They win Nobel Prizes (1979, 1992)
4. Theory becomes "canonical"
5. Gets taught in every econ PhD program
6. Entire careers built on extending it
7. Textbooks written around it
8. **Now embedded in the structure of the profession**

**The lock-in:**
- Can't admit the Nobel committees were wrong
- Can't rewrite all the textbooks
- Can't tell thousands of economists their careers are based on flawed theory
- Can't undermine the profession's legitimacy
- **Too many reputations at stake**

**Economic term:** **Sunk cost fallacy** - we've invested so much in this framework, we can't abandon it now

**Academic term:** **Paradigm defense** - the profession protects its core theories even in the face of contradictory evidence

---

### 3. **MATH = LEGITIMACY (Regardless of Validity)**

**The mechanism:**
In economics, theories gain status through mathematical sophistication, NOT empirical accuracy.

**Why HCT/EMH dominate:**
- They can be expressed mathematically
- Math makes them look "scientific"
- Math allows formal proofs
- Math is impressive and exclusive (most people can't critique it)
- **Math provides legitimacy independent of truth**

**The profession rewards:**
- Mathematical elegance > empirical fit
- Theoretical consistency > real-world accuracy
- Formal models > messy reality
- **"Rigor" (defined as math) > relevance**

**Quote from economist Paul Romer (2015):**
> "For more than three decades, macroeconomics has gone backwards... [economists] use a theory that is inconsistent with facts... because it allows them to be mathematically precise."

**The deeper issue:**
- Math allows you to APPEAR rigorous while being empirically wrong
- Non-mathematical critiques (like Nadal's) can be dismissed as "not economics"
- **Methodological gatekeeping protects bad theories**

---

### 4. **THE THEORIES ARE FLEXIBLE ENOUGH TO "EXPLAIN" ANYTHING (Unfalsifiable)**

**The problem:**
HCT and EMH are so flexible, they can accommodate any evidence.

**When HCT seems to fail:**

**Evidence:** "Black workers with same education earn less than white workers"

**HCT defenders respond:**
- "Ah, but their HC differs in unmeasured ways (school quality, soft skills, networks)"
- → Add more variables, theory still holds

**Evidence:** "Controlling for all that, gap persists"

**HCT defenders:**
- "Must be other unmeasured HC differences"
- → Theory becomes unfalsifiable

**When EMH seems to fail:**

**Evidence:** "2008 crash - markets were wildly irrational"

**EMH defenders:**
- "No, prices reflected the information available at the time"
- "The crash WAS the new information being incorporated"
- → Theory can't be wrong because any price movement is "information"

**Philosopher Karl Popper's point:**
A theory that can explain any outcome explains nothing. If no evidence could falsify the theory, it's not scientific - it's ideology.

**But economics profession accepts this** because it protects the core framework.

---

### 5. **ALTERNATIVE THEORIES ARE SYSTEMATICALLY MARGINALIZED**

**The mechanism:**
Theories that challenge HCT/EMH are kept at the margins of the profession.

**Examples:**

**Structural racism theories:**
- Explain wage gaps by discrimination, not "HC differences"
- Evidence: audit studies showing identical resumes get different responses by race
- **But**: Can't be formalized in same mathematical framework
- **Result**: Dismissed as "sociology, not economics"

**Behavioral economics:**
- Shows systematic irrationality (contradicts EMH)
- Evidence: Kahneman/Tversky experiments (Kahneman won Nobel 2002)
- **But**: Treated as "quirks" or "anomalies," not fundamental challenge
- **Result**: Absorbed as "behavioral biases" but EMH core remains

**Institutional economics:**
- Emphasizes power, history, institutions (not just individual choice)
- **But**: Can't be reduced to optimization models
- **Result**: Marginalized as "heterodox"

**The gatekeeping:**
- Top journals reject papers that don't use mainstream framework
- PhD programs don't train students in alternatives
- Tenure committees punish deviation from orthodox methods
- **Alternative theories can't gain traction** even with better empirical fit

**The result:**
You can challenge HCT/EMH at the margins, but the core framework remains dominant because alternatives are structurally excluded.

---

### 6. **ECONOMICS AS IDEOLOGY, NOT SCIENCE (The Uncomfortable Truth)**

**The claim:**
Economics isn't actually an empirical science - it's a form of political ideology that uses math to claim scientific status.

**Evidence:**

**Compare to physics:**
- Physics: Theory predicts X, observe Y → abandon or modify theory
- Economics: Theory predicts X, observe Y → claim Y is actually consistent with X, or ignore Y

**EMH example:**
- Theory: Markets are efficient, bubbles shouldn't exist
- Reality: Bubbles happen repeatedly (2000, 2008, crypto)
- Response: "Those weren't bubbles" or "rational bubbles" or "EMH still valid in the long run"
- **Theory never actually abandoned**

**HCT example:**
- Theory: Investment in HC → proportional returns regardless of race
- Reality: Discrimination means returns vary by race
- Response: "There must be unmeasured HC differences"
- **Theory never actually tested, just asserted**

**Physicist Richard Feynman's critique:**
> "The first principle is that you must not fool yourself—and you are the easiest person to fool."

**Economics seems to violate this constantly:**
- Start with political conclusion (markets are efficient, poverty is choice)
- Build mathematical model that reaches that conclusion
- Dismiss evidence to the contrary
- Claim scientific neutrality
- **Ideology with equations**

---

### 7. **PROFESSIONAL INCENTIVES REWARD CONFORMITY, NOT TRUTH**

**The academic incentive structure:**

**What gets rewarded:**
- Publishing in top journals (which enforce orthodox methods)
- Citing canonical works (including HCT/EMH)
- Extending existing frameworks (not challenging them)
- Mathematical sophistication (regardless of empirical fit)
- **Fitting in > being right**

**What gets punished:**
- Fundamental critiques of core theories
- Interdisciplinary work (sociology, history)
- Emphasizing empirical failures
- Questioning the profession's assumptions
- **Challenging orthodoxy costs you your career**

**The result:**
- Young economists learn to work within the paradigm
- Critical voices leave the profession or are pushed out
- Those who succeed are those who accept the framework
- **Selection bias: the profession selects for believers**

**Sociologist Pierre Bourdieu's concept:**
"Symbolic violence" - the dominated internalize the framework of the dominators. Economists internalize that HCT/EMH are "serious economics" and alternatives are not.

---

## The Synthesis: Why Failed Theories Dominate

**It's not one reason - it's a system:**

1. **Political utility** - serves powerful interests
2. **Institutional lock-in** - Nobel Prizes, textbooks, careers
3. **Methodological gatekeeping** - math = legitimacy
4. **Unfalsifiability** - flexible enough to "explain" anything
5. **Marginalization of alternatives** - structural exclusion
6. **Ideology not science** - starts with conclusions, works backwards
7. **Professional incentives** - conformity rewarded, dissent punished

**These reinforce each other:**
- Theory serves power → Power funds research → Research gets published → Theory becomes canonical → Institutions defend it → Math makes it exclusive → Alternatives marginalized → Young economists learn it → They defend it → Cycle continues

**The system is self-reproducing.**
