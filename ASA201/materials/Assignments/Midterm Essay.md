# Midterm Essay

**Points:** 50.0


## Description

ASA201/Introduction to Asian American Studies/Midterm

 

Choose a key term or concept from the list below and use it to discuss a “cultural text” of your choice. Examples of Asian American cultural texts include: music, poetry, new media, architecture, video games. The key is to argue clearly what the text shows its readers about that key term—its complexities, its contradictions, its implications, its imaginings—and how does it do so? What are the formal, aesthetic, or rhetorical strategies that the cultural text uses to forward that understanding of the key term? **You must run your chosen cultural text by me prior to the midterm.**

Grading will focus, in part, on originality of your analysis. You are encouraged to draw connections and “mix” a cultural text with a concept or key term.

 

Your paper should be thesis-driven (and your thesis is your interpretation of how the text engages that key term). Your total page count should be about 4-5 pages, handwritten, double-spaced. 

 

Successful essays will be argumentative and analyze specific images or instances from the theoretical and cultural texts to support claims but does **not **require the use of direct quotations since you will be writing the essay from memory. Do note that the final essay can be a revision/expansion of this midterm essay, so take some time to choose a concept and cultural text in which you feel genuinely interested.

 

The midterm essay is not one that requires outside research beyond the theoretical essays that you have read for class. You may want to draw on one of these theoretical texts to frame your essay, but the bulk of your essay should be spent on your critical interpretation of the cultural text.
