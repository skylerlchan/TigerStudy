### Expected Utility Applications

- Value of life estimation through risky choices
- Package delivery scenario with small accident risk- Upper bound calculation: L < Δ/p (life value < payment/probability)- Risk averse individuals require positive expected wealth change
- Mathematical proof using expected utility maximization
- Intuitive method: must gain money to compensate for risk- Formal approach: EU(risky) ≥ EU(safe) with concave utility function

### Risk Measurement & Concepts

- Variance as risk measure
- Formula 1: E[X²] - (E[X])²- Formula 2: Average squared deviation from mean
- Certainty Equivalent (CE): lump sum making you indifferent to gamble
- Example: Melanie’s CE = $25 for 50/50 chance of losing $100 or gaining $200
- Risk Premium: amount willing to sacrifice to eliminate risk
- Melanie’s risk premium = $50 - $25 = $25- Always positive for risk averse individuals

### Risk Aversion Coefficients

- Absolute Risk Aversion: -U’‘(w)/U’(w)
- CARA utility: U(w) = -e^(-αw)/α- Constant across wealth levels
- Relative Risk Aversion: -wU’‘(w)/U’(w)
- CRRA utility: U(w) = w^(1-γ)/(1-γ)- Constant coefficient γ

### Portfolio Theory Application

- Two asset example:
- Safe asset: 5% return, zero risk- Risky asset: 20% expected return, 12% standard deviation
- Target: $15 profit on $100 investment (15% return)
- Solution: 1/3 in safe asset, 2/3 in risky asset- Overall risk: 8% standard deviation
- General formula: Expected return = α(RF) + (1-α)(RR)
