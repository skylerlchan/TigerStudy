## Key Reading Concepts - EXPECT QUESTIONS!

### 1. Monson - "Saying Something" (Week 5)
**Concept:** How jazz musicians communicate meaning through playing
- Musical conversation and interaction
- Emotional depth and cultural references
- Expressing identity through improvisation

### 2. Berliner - "Thinking in Jazz" (Week 6)
**Concept:** Jazz improvisation as a learned language
- Internalized vocabulary of patterns and phrases
- Mental library of musical ideas
- "A Very Structured Thing" - improvisation has grammar
- Spontaneous recall and recombination

### 3. Szwed - "1959: Multiple Revolutions" (Week 6)
**Concept:** Why 1959 was revolutionary
- *Kind of Blue* introduced modal jazz
- First takes with minimal rehearsal
- Changed jazz forever
