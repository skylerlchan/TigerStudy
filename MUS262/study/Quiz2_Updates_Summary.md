## Sample Three-Week Comparison Questions

**Q: How does Hard Bop differ from Modal Jazz? (Two ways)**
A:
1. Harmony: Hard Bop uses complex chord changes; Modal Jazz uses minimal harmony
2. Rhythm: Hard Bop emphasizes groove; Modal Jazz has spacious, floating feel

**Q: Why did Modal Jazz emerge from Hard Bop?**
A: Musicians sought freedom from complex chord progressions. Modal jazz simplified harmony by using modes/scales, allowing more space for melodic exploration.

**Q: What are two ways Free Jazz broke from Modal Jazz?**
A:
1. Structure: Modal jazz still used modes; Free jazz abandoned all structures
2. Harmony: Modal jazz had static harmony; Free jazz had no harmony (atonal)
