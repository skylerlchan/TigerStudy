## Key Reading Concepts to Know

### Monson - "Saying Something" (Week 5)
- What does it mean to "say something" in jazz?
- Musical conversation and interaction
- Cultural identity in jazz performance

### Berliner - "Thinking in Jazz" (Week 6)
- How jazz musicians learn improvisation
- The vocabulary of jazz (patterns, phrases, harmonic concepts)
- "A Very Structured Thing" - improvisation as a language with grammar

### Szwed Chapters
- **Chapter 20 (Hard Bop):** Soul jazz, gospel influence, funk elements
- **Chapter 22 (Post-Bebop Improvisation):** Evolution after bebop
- **Chapter 23 (1959: Multiple Revolutions):** *Kind of Blue*, modal jazz breakthrough
- **Chapter 24 (Free Jazz):** Breaking all conventions, Ornette Coleman's innovations
- **Chapter 26 (The 1970s):** Context for where free jazz led
