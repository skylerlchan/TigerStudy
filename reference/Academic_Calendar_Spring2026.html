Critical Stretches

  • March 5–6: ECO Midterm (Thu) + ORF Midterm 1 + MUS 5-page paper both on Fri — your hardest 48 hours of the semester. Start studying 2+ weeks in advance.
  • May 12: ORF Final (12:30pm) immediately followed by ECO Final (4pm) — two quantitative finals back-to-back on the same day.
  • May 9–10: ASA Final Essay (Sat noon) then MUS 10-page research paper (Sun 7pm) on consecutive days. The MUS paper requires research — don't leave it for the last week.
  • ORF 309 HWs: Due every Wednesday at 11:59pm — no exceptions without documentation. Build the routine now.
  • ASA 201 Canvas posts: 23 posts across the semester, every Mon & Wed by 9pm. Skipping even a few will sting your grade (20% of final grade).
