# MUS 262 Midterm Paper Requirements

- **Length:** 5 pages, double-spaced
- **Due:** Friday, March 6, 2026
- **Weight:** 20% of final grade
- **Format:** MLA citation format
- **Topic:** An artist of jazz or a movement of jazz, roughly covering 1900-1965 (Early Jazz through Modal Jazz)
- **Topic approval required** by the professor
- **Scope:** Fairly open-ended — could be a critique of an album, an artist profile with stylistic analysis, or a critical/supportive analysis of an artist's or movement's contributions
- **Sources:** Should incorporate academic primary sources
- **Advice:** Pick something you feel most passionate about from what was covered in class
