Dear all,

Below is the exam policy and logistics for Midterm 1. **Please read this information carefully and in its entirety**. The exam policy will be strictly enforced.

**MIDTERM 1 EXAM LOGISTICS**

- The exam will take place during **Friday****, Mar 06 9:35 am - 10:25 am Eastern Time in person** in **Friend Center 101**.

- You have at most **50**** minutes** to work the exam problems.

- Please **arrive at least 10 minutes early** so we can start promptly.

- All questions must be answered in the exam booklet.

- Do not forget to write your name on your booklet.

- You must also return the question pages to us at the end of the exam.

- Students who have ODS accommodations will receive a separate email from us.

**EXAM POLICY**

*1. Honor code*

- You must write the honor code pledge at the beginning. **Exams lacking the honor code pledge will not be graded.**

- ****You are expected to be familiar with and obey the **Princeton Honer System** and **all** the exam policy in this announcement. You will not be reminded of these points in the text of the exam, but any violation will nonetheless be considered an honor code violation. 

*2. Allowed materials*

- You are allowed a **hand-written cheat sheet **consisting of** a double-sided (standard letter size) page**. The cheat sheet must be hand-written, not typed (this limits the amount of information you can fit). You may discuss the cheat sheet with other students prior to the exam, but each student is expected to prepare her/his own cheat sheet.

- You must hand in your cheat sheet** **with the exam solutions**.** 

- ****You are **not** allowed to use any book or notes during the exam other than your cheat sheet. You are also **not** allowed to use a calculator.

- ****You are **not** allowed to collaborate with any other student during the exam, or to make use of any other resources beside the cheat sheet.

- You **cannot** use any electronic devices in any way during the exam.

*3. Covered material and grading*

- Midterm 1 covers **all the material up to and including section 2.3 of the lecture notes (#9 of the lecture slides)**.

- As usual, it important to justify all your answers carefully. It is not enough just to have the right answer: you must convince us that you got the right answer for the right reasons. **Giving a correct answer without adequate justification will cost points on the exam.**

- Midterm 1 is worth 20% of your final grade.

Please let us know if there are any further questions or concerns, and best of luck on the exam!
