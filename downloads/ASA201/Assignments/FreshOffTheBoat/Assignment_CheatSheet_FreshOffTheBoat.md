## YOUR MAIN ARGUMENT (memorize this)

**Fresh Off the Boat's transformation from Eddie Huang's raw memoir to ABC sitcom reveals the terms of differential inclusion—Asian Americans get representation BUT only if anger is removed, politics are sanitized, and racism becomes "cute." Huang's public disavowal demonstrates strategic anti-essentialism: using "Asian American" to gain access while refusing to let it settle into fixed representation.**

---

## KEY CONCEPTS + DEFINITIONS

**Strategic Essentialism** (Lowe via Spivak)
- "Strategic use of positive essentialism in a scrupulously visible political interest"
- Use identity category to gain access/visibility
- BUT don't let it become fixed/reified
- Huang did this: memoir → TV deal (strategic) → public critique (anti-essentialist)

**Aestheticizing Commodification** (Lowe)
- "Aestheticizing commodification of Asian American cultural differences while denying the immigrant histories of material exclusion"
- Taking MATERIAL realities (violence, poverty, racism) and making them AESTHETIC (cute, consumable, pretty)
- The adaptation did this: memoir's violence → TV's "quirky misunderstandings"

**Differential Inclusion** (your list)
- You're included BUT on limited terms that don't threaten the system
- Asian Americans on TV IF they're not angry, confirm American dream, stay "safe"

**Collaborative Antagonism** (Chuh)
- "Working together AND working subversively against"
- Huang: worked WITH ABC (made show) AND AGAINST it (public critique)

**Hegemony** (Lowe via Gramsci)
- Not just domination but "entire process of dissent and compromise"
- Sitcom genre = hegemonic apparatus (naturalizes dominant ideology)

## FORMAL STRATEGIES TO ANALYZE

**Sitcom Genre:**
- 22-minute episodes with resolution
- Conflict → harmony by end
- Family as unit (privatizes politics)
- Can't sustain anger or critique

**Laugh Track:**
- Tells viewers when to laugh
- Makes racism "funny" not violent
- Teaches how to consume difference
- "Racial pedagogy"

**Food as Symbol:**
- Visual shorthand for "culture"
- Reduces complexity to consumable image
- Lunchbox scene = aestheticizing commodification

## AVOID THESE MISTAKES

❌ "The show is good/bad representation"
✅ "The show reveals how representation functions within power"

❌ "This scene shows Asian culture"
✅ "This scene aestheticizes/commodifies cultural difference"

❌ "Huang was just bitter"
✅ "Huang's critique enacts strategic anti-essentialism"

❌ Only analyzing content
✅ Analyzing FORM (genre, structure, laugh track)
