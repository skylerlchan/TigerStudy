## Course-Specific Notes

### ECO 310 -- Midterm (Mar 5, 24-30%)

- **Highest-weight midterm.** Gets priority in all scheduling conflicts.
- Problem-set-first studying. Redo PS1 and PS2 from scratch before Practice 1.
- **Attend the Mar 3 review session** -- professors often telegraph what's on the exam.
- Last 48 hours: no new material, only practice problems and review of mistakes.

### ORF 309 -- Midterm 1 (Mar 6, 20%)

- HW problems are the exam prep. Redo every problem you lost points on (HW1-HW5).
- Check if a formula sheet is provided. If not, build your own by Mar 4 -- the act of building it is itself studying.
- Stagger practices with ECO so you're never doing two timed practice exams the same morning.

### ASA 201 -- Midterm Essay (Feb 26, 20%)

- In-class essay -- speed and structure matter.
- Your 20+ Canvas discussion posts are a built-in study guide. Re-read them.
- Have 2-3 flexible essay outlines ready for different prompts.

### MUS 262 -- Midterm Paper (Mar 6, 20%)

- 5 pages. This is a writing assignment, not an exam -- start it Feb 27 and finish by Mar 5 night.
- Must be completely done before you go to sleep Mar 5. If it bleeds into Mar 6 morning, it compromises ORF revision.

## Non-Negotiables

1. **ECO + ORF prep starts today.** Both get the full 14 days. Content review first, then practices spaced every 3-4 days, then revision.
2. **Every practice is timed and closed-book.** Set a timer, sit down, do it for real.
3. **Review every practice the next day.** Redo every problem you got wrong.
4. **MUS paper done by Mar 5 night.** Non-negotiable.
5. **Don't skip the weekly grind:** ORF HW (Wed 11:59pm) and ASA Canvas posts (Mon/Wed 9pm) are free points.
6. **Protect sleep on Mar 4 and Mar 5.** Two back-to-back exam days on no sleep costs more points than extra cramming gains.
