## Discussion Prompt

Prompt: Puar makes a wide-ranging argument that there are embedded terms and concepts within US discourses of queerness and sexuality that shape also how the West represents in an Orientalist manner "the Other" in the (Middle) East.

Fundamentally, she asks us to question what it means to associate Western queerness with concepts of liberation, freedom, "coming out", authenticity in being one's "true self", etc. while representing the Oriental "Other" in the (Middle) East. as repressed and repressive within a deviant and "terroristic" sexuality.

How does this focus on sexuality and gender as a dividing point between East and West build on *and complicate* our previous discussions on the relationship of the US and the rest of the world, especially in terms of justification for intervention into "backwards" nations in need of "help"?
