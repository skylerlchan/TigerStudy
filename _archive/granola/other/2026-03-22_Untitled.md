## Summary

### Musical Leadership and Spiritual Direction

- Conductor’s role as vessel for larger work
- Point to something beyond self- Champion group strengths while interpreting composer’s vision- Allow performers and audience to be suspended in time
- Outside stress disrupts ensemble unity
- Unwillingness to submit to larger team perspective- Individual ideas must contribute to collective whole
- Universal accessibility principle
- Anyone can sing, like gratitude and cooking- Everyone’s contributions make music truly compelling and honest

### Prophetic Ministry Through Musical Metaphor

- Prophets as spiritual conductors
- Help listeners engage more receptively with Christ- Interpret and elevate teachings rather than replace Christ- Create Christ-like disciples, not celebrity followers
- Nephi’s prophetic mission statement: “We talk of Christ, we rejoice in Christ, we preach of Christ, we prophesy of Christ”
- Receiving prophetic counsel requires faith and patience
- D&C 21: “Give heed unto all his words…as if from mine own mouth”- D&C 138: “Whether by mine own voice or by the voice of my servants, it is the same”
- 12th century bishop’s organ metaphor
- Diverse pipes creating single melody through shared air- Spiritual harmony from discordant elements unified by common purpose- Material embodiment of cosmic harmony as teaching tool

### Faith as Choice and Disposition

- Multiple definitions of faith create confusion
- Believing IN Christ vs believing CHRIST (Stephen Robinson distinction)- Faith as gift from heaven that comes through choosing to believe- Faith as disposition rather than evidence-based conviction
- Parable of the sower application
- Seed (word) growth depends on soil condition (heart disposition)- Same content yields different results based on receiver’s willingness- Active agency required in faith cultivation process
- Evidence limitations in belief formation
- Korihor and Laman/Lemuel examples show signs don’t guarantee faith- Political discourse demonstrates evidence’s limited persuasive power- Choice to believe precedes and enables spiritual growth
- Alma 32 principle: Desire to believe IS exercising faith
- Not placeholder for future “real” faith- Disposition to believe constitutes faith itself- Cultivating receptive spiritual soil enables word to take root
