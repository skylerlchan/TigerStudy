### FedTech Business Model & Approach

- Revenue from government contracts to run accelerator programs

- Recruits founding teams of 3 people typically:
- Business/hustle archetype- Technical background relevant to technology- Mixed skills person

- 6-9 month process from invention to startup formation

### Deep Tech Founder Success Traits

- Creative funding approaches beyond traditional VC
- Government grants (especially SBIR)- Business competitions- Angel investors
- Market flexibility - ability to pivot between adjacent applications
- Willingness to shut down non-viable technologies quickly
- Passion develops through understanding use cases, not necessarily pre-existing

### Student Founder Challenges & Advice

- Gauri’s carbon credit AI agent dilemma: product improvement vs customer outreach
- Recommendation: Be intentional about focus periods- Sprint approach: 3 weeks of 100 customer conversations, then development pause
- Finding customers beyond initial network
- LinkedIn outreach with AI assistance- Don’t wait for warm introductions- Rejection now doesn’t prevent future engagement
- Timing while at Princeton
- Episodic approach more effective than daily hours- Summer intensives and semester breaks- Hardware projects require longer timelines than software

### Government & Enterprise Sales Insights

- SBIR grants: $3-4B annually, Phase 1 ($150K), Phase 2 ($1M)
- Government more accessible than perceived
- Reach out to program managers directly- Venture groups at large companies (Roche, P&G, Nike, Samsung)- Brute force networking approach works
- IP licensing from research institutions
- Universities/NASA own patents, license to startups- Researcher buy-in crucial even without ownership- Tech transfer office makes licensing decisions
