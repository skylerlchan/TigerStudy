## Summary

### Slow Ventures Overview

- Early-stage VC fund focused on pre-revenue companies
- Typically first money raised by founders
- ~50% of deals are pre-incorporation
- Check sizes: $1-4M range
- Portfolio: ~300-400 companies
- Investment criteria:
- Exceptional people they’re excited to spend time with- Big, important stories/trends- Weird takes on those stories- Terminal value potential

### Investment Philosophy & Approach

- Focus on people and ideas over revenue metrics
- “Weird takes on important stories” - unique approaches to major trends
- Key trends they’re tracking:
- AI diffusion into real world- GLP-1 drugs (major healthcare breakthrough)- Deglobalization/decline of liberal world order- US demographic cliff- Climate change
- Emphasis on “cheating” - finding unfair advantages
- Young founders have advantage of being able to “look stupid” and fail

### Team Backgrounds & Hiring

- Speaker backgrounds:
- Started as corporate paralegal, worked on Uber IPO- 15 years at Lowenstein law firm in Palo Alto- Pre-med to consulting (BCG) to VC path- 4 years at Slow, previously at another VC fund
- Hiring process example:
- 10pm email for 7:30am breakfast next day- Eliminated candidates who didn’t show up- Assessed room dynamics and charisma- Case study analysis
- Looking for: high agency, analytical rigor, relationship building, willingness to work hard

### Current Market Perspective

- Exceptional time to build startups vs. work in VC/finance
- Speed of execution unprecedented
- Three key roles at early companies:
- Product engineer/slop cannon (high velocity, good judgment)- Adults (governance, risk assessment, security)- Hot people (culture, energy, relationship building)
- Advice: join startups rather than investment firms currently

### Legal & Compliance Insights

- AI creating massive legal question volume for clients
- Models still make errors despite improvements
- Companies moving so fast they’re “built on foundations of sand”
- General advice: do bare minimum compliance unless in regulated industry
- Incorporation guidance:
- Use standard structures (Delaware C-Corp)- Don’t get creative with legal structures- Use Clerky or Stripe Atlas for formation- Safe notes for <$7M raises, price rounds for larger

### Equity & Founding Team Dynamics

- Founding engineer equity discussion with Skyler:
- 2-3 founders already in YC- Skyler transitioning from intern to full-time- Recommended 2-5% equity range for founding engineer role- Standard 4-year vesting with cliff- Risk/reward asymmetry noted for founding engineers
